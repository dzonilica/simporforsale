The Process

Six steps to the right dog.

A structured, transparent placement process from your first call to long-term support. No surprises, no hidden fees, no dog placed until you are ready to take responsibility for it.

01

Initial consultation

A private 30–45 minute call. We discuss your lifestyle, household, property, travel patterns, family composition (including children and other pets), your experience with working dogs, and, most importantly, what protection actually means for you.

This call is diagnostic. Half the time we recommend a program you did not initially ask about. A small fraction of the time, we recommend that you do not buy a Simpor dog at all.

02

Meet & evaluate the dog

You come to Simpor in Serbia, or we arrange a private extended video walk-through if travel is impossible. Either way you see the dog work, obedience, protection, environmental exposure, temperament with strangers, and interaction with our own family.

You leave the visit knowing exactly which dog you would take home, and why.

03

Selection & agreement

Once you have chosen a dog, we prepare a written purchase agreement covering the dog, the training standard, what is included with the dog (pedigree, health certificate, x-rays, vaccinations, microchip), the placement schedule, handler training, and satisfaction guarantee.

Everything is written. Nothing is added later. You sign a document you fully understand.

04

Preparation for placement

In the weeks before placement we prepare you, not the dog. The dog is already fully trained. What we work on with you: your home setup, your daily routine, the command word system, who else in the family will handle the dog, and any environmental factors we should proof for beforehand.

05

Ongoing support

Placement is not the end. Refresher training visits, remote coaching by phone or video, replacement of any handler equipment, and long-term guidance remain open to you indefinitely.

Many of our clients now on their second Simpor dog have kept the same relationship for 10+ years. That is by design.

Timelines & Expectations

What to expect, and when.

A typical Simpor placement runs 4–10 weeks from first call to pickup, depending on the program, your travel schedule, and whether the specific dog you select is available now or requires a short waiting period for final proofing.

  • Week 1: Initial consultation call
  • Week 2–3: In-person evaluation or video walk-through
  • Week 3–4: Agreement signed, deposit received
  • Week 4–8: Final proofing, health documentation, travel prep
  • Week 8–10: Pickup at our facility in Serbia
  • Ongoing: Long-term support and refresher work

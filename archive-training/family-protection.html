The Foundation

Obedience is where every working dog begins.

Whether the goal is a stable family companion, a titled sport competitor, or a professional K9, the foundation is the same. Reliable obedience is what separates a dog that can be trusted anywhere from a dog that can only be trusted in familiar places.

At Simpor Dog Center, obedience is offered as a full curriculum in three progressive levels, open to all breeds and all ages. Each level is taught with the same technique used to prepare our IGP competitors, the difference is only how far the training goes.

Level 01

Basic Obedience.

The focus of this training is on basic commands, proper leash walking, socialization, and reducing unwanted behavior. This level creates the foundation for further work and allows owners to communicate with their dog more easily and confidently in everyday life.

  • - All breeds, all ages
  • - Basic commands: sit, down, stay, come
  • - Proper leash walking
  • - Socialization & behavior correction
Level 02

Advanced, Tricks & Improvements.

The advanced obedience level is intended for dogs that have already completed the basic and intermediate stages. The program focuses on further developing control, creative exercises, tricks, and advanced commands that improve the dog's concentration, flexibility, and working motivation. Ideal for owners wanting a higher level of communication and mental stimulation.

  • - Advanced-level control
  • - Creative exercises & tricks
  • - Mental stimulation & motivation
  • - Precision under distraction
Level 03

BH / IGP Obedience.

This program is designed for dogs preparing for BH and IGP examinations in accordance with current international regulations. Training includes sport-level obedience work, precision in performing exercises, stability under pressure, and reliable work in different situations. Special emphasis is placed on correct technique and preparation for examination and competition.

  • - Sport-level obedience
  • - IGP 1 · 2 · 3 preparation
  • - Competition-ready precision
  • - FCI standards & examination technique
Included in Every Level

The Simpor obedience foundation.

Individual assessment

Every dog is evaluated before training begins, drive, temperament, prior experience, and the specific goals of the owner.

Structured progression

Each level builds directly on the last. No shortcuts, no rushed advancement, the dog moves forward only when the foundation is solid.

Handler involvement

Owners and handlers are trained alongside the dog, so the work continues at home with the same technique and consistency.

Certified professionals

Every session is conducted by certified trainers under the direct supervision of founder Dejan Simović.

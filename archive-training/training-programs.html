Training Services

Six categories. One standard.

From first obedience to World Championship IGP, from personal protection to police and military K9, six complete training categories built on three decades of professional working dog experience.

Our Approach

Precision. Drive. Results.

At Simpor Dog Center, every training program is built on decades of real-world experience in sport, protection, and K9 work. We do not follow trends, we set standards.

Each dog is assessed individually before beginning training. Programs are structured around the dog's natural drives, temperament, and the specific goals of the owner or handler. Whether you aim for World Championship competition or simply a reliable, well-behaved companion, the methodology is always the same: correct, consistent, and effective.

All training is conducted by certified professionals under the direct supervision of founder Dejan Simović, ensuring the highest possible standard in every session.

Category 01 · Obedience

Obedience, for every breed, at every level.

From basic foundations for family dogs to elite IGP competition preparation, our obedience programs cover every stage of development with precision and care.

01

Basic Obedience

Focus on basic commands, proper leash walking, socialization, and reducing unwanted behavior. Creates the foundation for further work and allows owners to communicate with their dog with confidence in everyday life.

02

Advanced, Tricks & Improvements

For dogs that have completed the basic and intermediate stages. Develops control, creative exercises, tricks, and advanced commands that improve concentration, flexibility, and working motivation.

BH / IGP Obedience

Prepared according to current international IGP regulations. Sport-level obedience, precision, stability under pressure, and reliable performance, with emphasis on correct technique and examination preparation.

Protection & K9 Work.

From personal protection and sport-level bite-work to police K9 preparation and breeding licenses, professional programs for serious working dog goals.

01

Personal Protection

Category 04 · BH / IGP Foundation

The complete working dog, in three phases.

The BH confirms your dog's readiness for life and work. IGP builds the complete working dog, from tracking and obedience to protection.

BH

BH, Companion Dog Test

Basic obedience and socialization test for dogs living and working in urban environments. Confirms stable temperament, control, obedience, and proper behavior around people, other dogs, and traffic. Prerequisite for all further working and sport titles.

IGP

IGP, Working Dog Foundation

The foundation of professional work with working dogs and the basis for further sport development. High-level obedience, command precision, and stability under demanding conditions, directed toward sport competition or professional work.

A

Phase A, Tracking

The dog uses its nose to find and follow a human track, demonstrating concentration, precision, and calmness while correctly indicating found articles. Develops mental stability, focus, and independence.

B

Phase B, Obedience

Precise execution of exercises under the handler's control, heeling, changes of pace, recalls, stays. Accuracy, concentration, and harmony between dog and handler, targeting maximum points under sport conditions.

C

Phase C, Protection

Evaluates the dog's courage, determination, and control in attack and defense situations. Strong working drives, correct technique, high-level control and obedience, with strict respect for safety and sport standards.

Category 05 · Breeding Licenses

KKL & ZTP Breeding Licenses.

Breeding licenses that guarantee the quality and future of the breed, prepared to the highest international standard.

KKL

KKL (Körung), German Shepherd Breeding License

The official breeding license for German Shepherd Dogs, conducted according to the rules of the Verein für Deutsche Schäferhunde (SV). Evaluation of genetic potential, temperament stability, anatomy, and working abilities. Only dogs meeting the highest criteria receive a breeding license.

ZTP

ZTP, Breeding Suitability Test for Working Breeds

Breeding suitability test for Rottweilers, Dobermans, and other working breeds according to the regulations of their respective breed organizations. Evaluates temperament, stability, working drives, obedience, and reaction under protection and stress, the standard for responsible, professional breeding.

Category 06 · K9 Programs

Professional K9 Programs.

From search and detection to police and military service, complete K9 programs built on 30 years of professional working dog experience.

01

K9 Searching

Search and detection work in different environments. Scent work, concentration, and precision in locating the target, reliable performance in both controlled and real-life conditions.

02

K9 Civil

Adapted for dogs working in everyday and urban environments. Stability, obedience, and proper reaction when exposed to people, traffic, and various external factors.

03

K9 Protection

Controlled protection work with a clear emphasis on obedience, stability, and safe, reliable responses in real-life situations. Designed for both professional use and high-stakes personal protection.

04

K9 Police Service

Training for police service dogs, obedience, protection work, and specialized tasks according to official requirements. Adapted for professional use and high working standards in law enforcement environments.

05

K9 Military Service

Dogs prepared for work in demanding and high-risk environments. High level of discipline, stress resistance, and reliable performance in complex situations where there is no margin for error.

06

K9 Security Service

Training for private security dogs, protection of people, property, and facilities. Combines obedience, controlled protection work, and stable behavior in a professional commercial security environment.

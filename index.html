The Difference You Can See

Three decades of working dogs, refined one dog at a time.

Every dog we place is bred, evaluated, and prepared under one roof, from bloodlines we've spent thirty years perfecting.

Founded in 1993 and registered with the FCI under number 3428, our kennel specializes in working-line dogs bred for temperament, nerve strength, and function, not paperwork alone. Our approach is quality over quantity, always.

  • FCI 3428 registered working kennel, Serbia
  • IPO/IGP-titled parents, hip & elbow certified
  • Master breeder with 30+ years of experience
  • Dogs raised in-home with children and family pets

Why Simpor

What sets Simpor apart.

Three decades of breeding and raising working dogs under one roof, a standard defined by the dogs we produce, not the promises we make.

  • Three generations of expertise

    A family kennel with over 30 years of continuous work, knowledge, bloodlines, and standards refined generation after generation.

  • Proven working bloodlines

    Internationally titled parents with verified pedigrees, dogs selected for stable nerves, focus, and drive to work under real pressure.

  • A name recognized across continents

    The Simpor name is respected in the working-dog world from Europe to the Middle East and beyond, built over decades of dogs that spoke for themselves.

  • Tested under real-world pressure

    Traffic, crowds, restaurants, elevators, gunfire, strangers, night scenarios. A Simpor dog is proofed in the environments it will actually live in.

  • The standard others try to match

    When elite families, professionals, and connoisseurs of the breed look for the top of the working line, Simpor is the address they come to.

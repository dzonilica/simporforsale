About Simpor Dog Center

30+ years of passion,
discipline, and excellence.

Simpor Dog Center is a family-run kennel and professional training facility, built on three decades of international achievement in working and sport dogs. Founded 1994 · FCI 3428 · Von Simpor Kennel · Serbia · Europe · World.

The Record

Simpor Kennel, FCI 3428, since 1994.

Simpor is a family kennel registered under FCI 3428, active since 1994. We work with German Shepherds, Dobermans, Rottweilers, Malinois and other working breeds, all under one roof, from a puppy's first day to the day it ships.

Est. 1994

Three decades of continuous work with German Shepherds and working breeds, one family, one philosophy, one uninterrupted craft.

FCI 3428

Registered with the Fédération Cynologique Internationale under kennel number 3428. Continuously active since 1994.

20+ World Top 10 placements

Von Simpor dogs have finished in the Top 10 at World Championships more than twenty times, across nearly every continent.

1st Serbian Crufts winner

Paris Von Simpor, the first and only Serbian dog to win at Crufts, the world's most prestigious dog show.

Czech National Working Champion

Won the Czech National Working Championship with Kendy Z. Kraje Husitu, one of the toughest working titles in Europe.

K9 Police Certified Handler

Certified working decoy and K9 handler. Active in police K9 training, IGP working sport, and international selections including KKL, ZTP, and CTP.

The Founder

Dejan Simović.

Founder · Breeder · Trainer

Dejan Simović is an internationally recognized dog breeder and trainer with over 30 years of experience specializing in German Shepherds and working breeds. He founded the Von Simpor kennel, recognized worldwide for stable temperament, strong working drives, excellent anatomy, and reliability.

His work has placed more than 20 dogs in the Top 10 at World Championships, with victories across nearly every continent, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, South Korea, China, Mongolia, and the Americas. Paris Von Simpor became the first and only Serbian dog to win Crufts. Kendy Z. Kraje Husitu won the Czech National Working Championship.

Beyond the show ring and sport field, Dejan is a certified helper in IGP working sport and a certified K9 handler, active in police K9 training and international dog selections including KKL, ZTP, and CTP.

The Early Years

A career built from zero.

There was no inheritance, no shortcut, no imported reputation. Everything Simpor Dog Center represents today was built from the ground up, one dog, one championship, one hard-earned title at a time.

Three decades later, that same standard defines every dog that leaves this kennel: bred with intention, raised with discipline, and delivered with the reputation of a name built the hard way.

Our Mission

In Dejan's own words.

"For me, everything begins and ends with the German Shepherd. Understanding this breed, its drives, its temperament, its purpose, has been the work of my life."

The German Shepherd is a breed I've spent decades studying, training, and breeding. The focus has always been the same, a stable, capable, honest dog you can trust in sport, in work, and at home.

Over the years I've had the chance to share this work internationally, leading seminars for handlers and trainers, and placing Von Simpor dogs on nearly every continent.

Simpor Dog Center is built in the heart of Šumadija. Today we focus on four things: genetics, training, nutrition, and veterinary care. The mission is simple, the right dog for the right home, and honest guidance at every step.

Our Approach

Precision. Drive. Results.

Every training program at Simpor Dog Center is built on decades of real-world experience in sport, protection, and K9 work. We do not follow trends, we set standards.

Each dog is assessed individually before beginning training. Programs are structured around the dog's natural drives, temperament, and the specific goals of the owner or handler. Whether the goal is World Championship competition or a reliable, well-behaved companion, the methodology is always the same: correct, consistent, and effective.

All training is conducted by certified professionals under the direct supervision of founder Dejan Simović, ensuring the highest possible standard in every session.

FAQ

Answered honestly.

The most common questions we get from prospective Simpor clients, answered without marketing language and without hedging.

Where is Simpor located?

+

Our center is located in Serbia, in the heart of the Šumadija region. Visits are welcome by appointment.

Which breeds do you work with?

+

We specialize in German Shepherds and working breeds, but our training programs are open to dogs of all breeds and all ages.

How can I book training?

+

The easiest way is to reach out through our contact form, by phone, or by email. We will get back to you and schedule an initial conversation about your dog and your goals.

Can I visit the facility?

+

Yes. Visits are welcome by appointment. Get in touch and we will arrange a suitable time.

How can I get in touch?

+

You can reach us by phone, email, or through the contact form on our website. We respond to every serious inquiry.
